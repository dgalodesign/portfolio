## Context
Milena Cloud is a digital product by Seresco for human-resources management. I worked on it through QA-BIT for **10 months**: 4 of research and analysis of the product and audience, and 6 of design, prototyping and development follow-up.

## Challenge
The product was badly outdated. It needed deep usability and experience improvements and a refreshed interface. The learning curve was very steep: completing one process meant navigating many windows in different places, and repetitive, complex processes slowed productivity.

The guiding line was to optimize processes and build features that boosted productivity and made the product easier to use and learn.

## My role
I joined midway through the initial research stage, so I took part from the earliest phases: **User Persona, Journey Map, Benchmark, user-interview analysis and Focus Group**. Later I handled web design and prototyping, and the creation and prototyping of the **Design System**.

Product team: one Senior UX/UI Designer and me. Methodology: Design Thinking for product design and Scrum for team management. Tools: Figma, Notion and Jira.

## Process
**Research:** client workshops, review of the current product, benchmarking and interviews.

**UX:** user persona, sitemap, user flows, wireframing and prototyping.

**UI:** moodboard, look and feel, Design System and component prototyping.

**Development:** Design System handoff and testing of the built web app.
